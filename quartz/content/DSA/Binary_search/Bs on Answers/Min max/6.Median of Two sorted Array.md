**LeetCode:** [Median of Two Sorted Arrays - LeetCode 4](https://leetcode.com/problems/median-of-two-sorted-arrays/)

## Brute-force approach

Merge the two sorted arrays into one sorted array, then read the middle value or average the two middle values. The existing implementation uses this approach.

**Time complexity:** $O(n + m)$

**Space complexity:** $O(n + m)$

## Optimal approach: partition-based binary search

Binary search the partition position in the smaller array. Choose the matching partition in the larger array so the left half contains half of the combined elements. When the left boundaries are less than or equal to the right boundaries, the partition is correct and the median comes from those boundary values.

**Time complexity:** $O(log(min(n, m)))$

**Space complexity:** $O(1)$
